# HandPals

A web page that turns your hands into the controller. It watches both players'
hands through the webcam and turns them into music, drawings and games — a
child and a grown-up side by side at one screen. Nothing to wear, nothing to
hold, nothing to install.

**Play it:** open the site in Chrome on a laptop with a webcam and allow the
camera when asked. Good light helps; sit far enough back that both people's
hands are in the picture.

**Privacy:** the camera picture is processed on your own computer and is never
uploaded. The page loads its hand-tracking code from a CDN at start-up; no
images or video ever leave the browser.

The whole app is one self-contained file, `index.html`.
